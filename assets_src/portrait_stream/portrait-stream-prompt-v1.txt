Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one transparent pixel-art character portrait sprite atlas for a retro first-person shooter webcam HUD, exactly SIX columns and FIVE rows, 30 portraits total, on a perfectly regular grid with no outer margins, no cell borders, no text or labels. Landscape canvas ratio 6:5; every equal cell has the same centered head-and-upper-shoulders framing and a small transparent gap around the silhouette.
Subject: the same ORIGINAL fictional French male amateur video streamer aged about 36 in EVERY portrait: ordinary believable civilian, distinctive long slightly crooked nose, thick expressive dark eyebrows, short tousled dark brown hair, three-day beard, brown eyes, worn olive zip hoodie over a muted rust-orange T-shirt. Confident, sarcastic, mildly paranoid personality; NOT Doomguy, not a soldier, no helmet, headset, glasses, weapons or props.
Style: deliberate 1990s hand-painted game portrait pixel art with hard chunky pixels, limited earthy palette, strong readable shapes and clean silhouette. The face is clearly readable at approximately 48 by 48 pixels. Warm skin with subtle green monitor bounce light, identical lighting across cells. No smooth photorealism, no vector shapes, no checkerboard drawn in the image; transparent background.
Layout exact: six expressions left-to-right on EVERY row: (1) neutral alert, lips closed and calm self-assurance; (2) taking a hit, eyes squinting and mouth grimacing; (3) firing in concentration, teeth clenched and eyebrows tense; (4) victory, smug wide grin; (5) discovering a secret, eyebrows raised and surprised delighted eyes; (6) speaking directly to the stream camera, mouth open mid-sentence with conversational expression.
Rows top-to-bottom represent five health states: (1) 80-100%, clean healthy confident; (2) 60-79%, small cheek scrape, slightly dishevelled; (3) 40-59%, split lip and visible cheek bruise; (4) 20-39%, prominent bruises, blood on brow and cheek, exhaustion; (5) 1-19%, pale, one eye swollen, visibly battered and frightened but alive. Tasteful stylized injuries only, no gore, no exposed bone. Every expression must keep its row's injuries and the SAME recognizable face, hairstyle, outfit and scale.
Production constraints: make precisely 30 cells in 6 columns by 5 rows, identical alignment, portrait top and bottom fully contained inside each cell, forehead and chin never cut off, health degradation increases down the columns and expression changes across rows. Background fully transparent. No lettering, watermark, decorative panels or UI. This atlas is for an art-direction proposal and webcam readability preview.
